# Large Batch 35 — 3D Procedural Visibility, Skyline, and Road-Continuity QA

Date: 2026-09-24

## Scope

This batch targets a high-priority gap left after Batches 20/24/26: automated semantic QA for generated 3D worlds, especially occlusion, skyline/landmark readability, and road continuity. Historical context was cross-checked against Gaming Alexandria holdings spanning Technopolis (1986/1987/1994), Play Meter (1988/1990), PC Engine Fan (1988–1997 complete run), and Famimaga 64 (1996–1998 complete run). These are used only as historical comparison points for changing expectations around navigation, 3D readability, course recognition, public play, and constrained hardware; no magazine text or imagery is reproduced here.

Modern technical anchors include Unity occlusion-culling documentation, Unreal screenshot/automation testing, and current road-network generation/evaluation research using connectivity, cycles, intersections, dead ends and graph cut-edges. The purpose is not to copy any source's wording, but to turn recurring concerns into reusable generator predicates and playtest questions.

## 1. A road can look continuous while being topologically broken

**Observation:** Rendered asphalt may visually meet across tile/chunk boundaries while lane metadata, navigation edges, collision or directionality remain disconnected.

**Mechanism:** Different representations are assembled or transformed independently; image continuity masks graph discontinuity.

**Generalized Principle:** Road continuity is a multi-layer invariant, not a screenshot property.

**Transfer:** At every seam verify render centerline proximity, lane endpoint pairing, nav-graph adjacency, collision continuity, legal direction and elevation/slope compatibility.

**Playtest Question:** Can an agent traverse every visually implied connection without teleporting, reversing illegally, leaving the drivable surface or hitting an invisible seam?

## 2. Global connectivity needs redundancy metrics, not only reachability

**Observation:** A road graph can be fully connected yet contain one fragile bridge edge whose failure or blockage isolates a large region.

**Mechanism:** Binary reachability ignores graph structure and alternate-route capacity.

**Generalized Principle:** Measure connectivity margin as well as connectivity.

**Transfer:** Record connected components, articulation points, bridges/cut-edges, cycle count, alternate-route ratio and detour factor for generated road networks.

**Playtest Question:** If the most important single road segment is blocked, how much playable space becomes unreachable and how large is the required detour?

## 3. Geometry continuity and driving continuity are different

**Observation:** A connected road can still be effectively undrivable because curvature, grade, width or lane offset changes too abruptly.

**Mechanism:** Topological validators do not model vehicle dynamics or steering preview distance.

**Generalized Principle:** Validate traversal envelopes, not merely connected meshes.

**Transfer:** Sample centerline curvature/grade/width over distance and flag discontinuities relative to expected speed and vehicle turning/braking limits.

**Playtest Question:** Does a baseline driver complete the seam at intended speed without emergency steering or braking?

## 4. Occlusion correctness and navigation readability are separate goals

**Observation:** An object being correctly hidden by geometry says nothing about whether the player receives enough information to navigate.

**Mechanism:** Rendering occlusion asks “should this object be visible?” while level readability asks “when must this cue become visible?”

**Generalized Principle:** Add gameplay visibility contracts on top of renderer visibility.

**Transfer:** Define critical landmarks/objectives with approach corridors and minimum visibility windows; ray/depth-test them from canonical route samples.

**Playtest Question:** Before a decision point, for how many meters/seconds is the intended landmark or branch cue actually visible?

## 5. Landmark visibility should be measured along trajectories

**Observation:** A landmark can be visible from a static showcase camera yet disappear for nearly the entire route players actually use.

**Mechanism:** Camera placement strongly changes occlusion; isolated screenshots sample the wrong distribution.

**Generalized Principle:** Visibility QA must be route-weighted and temporal.

**Transfer:** For each route sample record landmark visible fraction, longest occluded interval, first-visible distance, last-visible-before-junction distance and angular screen separation.

**Playtest Question:** Do common player trajectories preserve enough repeated sightings to build a stable mental map?

## 6. Occlusion flicker is a temporal failure

**Observation:** Thin poles, facade edges, LOD transitions or cell boundaries can make a landmark alternate visible/hidden over consecutive frames.

**Mechanism:** Binary per-frame tests miss unstable visibility near thresholds.

**Generalized Principle:** Treat visibility as a time series and test hysteresis/stability.

**Transfer:** Track visible-state transition rate and short visibility bursts; flag critical cues that chatter faster than a human can interpret.

**Playtest Question:** Does the cue remain visible long enough to identify, or does it flash between occluders/LODs?

## 7. Skyline diversity is structural, not pixel diversity

**Observation:** Decorative materials, weather and foliage can make screenshots differ while generated districts retain the same height rhythm and dominant silhouette.

**Mechanism:** RGB difference overweights surface noise and underweights large-scale geometry.

**Generalized Principle:** Compare skyline signatures in geometric/semantic space.

**Transfer:** From canonical viewpoints sample horizon elevation by azimuth, dominant landmark IDs, height-band occupancy, peak spacing and silhouette self-similarity.

**Playtest Question:** Can players distinguish districts from silhouette and massing before reading labels or textures?

## 8. A landmark must dominate at the right scale, not everywhere

**Observation:** Making a landmark permanently visible can improve orientation but flatten discovery and overwhelm local cues.

**Mechanism:** Global salience competes with neighborhood-scale navigation information.

**Generalized Principle:** Landmark visibility should have an intentional reveal schedule.

**Transfer:** Define far/mid/near visibility envelopes and expected reveal/occlusion zones instead of maximizing visible percentage globally.

**Playtest Question:** Does the landmark reappear at useful decision moments without becoming constant visual noise?

## 9. Camera semantics belong in procedural QA

**Observation:** The same generated geometry may pass a free-camera test but fail from gameplay camera height, FOV, pitch or vehicle offset.

**Mechanism:** Visibility is a function of geometry plus camera model.

**Generalized Principle:** Canonical QA cameras must match shipped gameplay semantics.

**Transfer:** Test walking/driving/drone cameras separately, including FOV/aspect/safe-area variants used on target devices.

**Playtest Question:** Does the generated world remain readable from the actual player camera rather than only editor views?

## 10. Visual regression should isolate semantic buffers

**Observation:** RGB screenshot diffs generate noise from lighting, particles and antialiasing while missing gameplay-significant depth/collision changes.

**Mechanism:** Presentation variance and gameplay geometry share the same image.

**Generalized Principle:** Compare multiple deterministic buffers and weight them by gameplay meaning.

**Transfer:** Capture RGB plus normalized depth, semantic class, road/lane mask, walkability/collision, landmark ID and occluder ID where feasible.

**Playtest Question:** When a visual diff fires, can QA tell whether the change is cosmetic, navigational, collision-related or topology-breaking?

## 11. Junction readability can be automatically stress-tested

**Observation:** Generated intersections often remain technically valid while branches are visually indistinguishable until after commitment.

**Mechanism:** Generator constraints ensure space but not pre-decision information.

**Generalized Principle:** Every consequential junction needs measurable preview separation.

**Transfer:** At stopping-distance samples compare branch heading, lane markings, width, elevation, destination landmark visibility and semantic identity; flag low-separation forks.

**Playtest Question:** Can a first-time player choose the intended branch before the safe steering/braking deadline?

## 12. Worst-case cameras are more valuable than random screenshots

**Observation:** Random viewpoints spend test budget on easy, redundant scenes while rare alignment failures remain hidden.

**Mechanism:** Severe occlusion and seam defects occupy narrow camera/route regions.

**Generalized Principle:** Search for adversarial viewpoints that minimize readability margins.

**Transfer:** Optimize camera samples for minimum landmark visibility, maximum branch ambiguity, maximum road-mask discontinuity or maximum semantic-depth disagreement; retain near-failures as regression fixtures.

**Playtest Question:** What camera/route position produces the smallest valid readability margin, and does that margin survive generator changes?

## Implementation-ready metric set

For each generated world/seed, store:

- road connected-component count
- lane endpoint mismatch count
- articulation points / graph bridges
- alternate-route ratio and detour factor
- curvature/grade/width discontinuity maxima
- baseline-agent route completion and intervention count
- landmark first-visible distance and visible-route fraction
- longest critical-landmark occlusion interval
- visibility transition/chatter rate
- pre-junction cue visibility time/distance
- branch semantic-separation score
- skyline azimuth signature / peak spacing / dominant landmark IDs
- canonical RGB/depth/semantic/road/collision hashes
- worst adversarial camera and failure margin

## Recommended CI pipeline

`GENERATE → GRAPH/LANE INVARIANTS → DYNAMICS ENVELOPE → CANONICAL ROUTES → GAMEPLAY-CAMERA CAPTURE → LANDMARK/JUNCTION VISIBILITY → SKYLINE SIGNATURE → SEMANTIC BUFFER DIFF → ADVERSARIAL CAMERA SEARCH → BASELINE AGENT DRIVE → WORST-N RETENTION → HUMAN REVIEW`

Use a cheap graph/geometry tier on every change, a canonical route/render tier on normal CI, and a larger adversarial seed/camera search on scheduled runs. Keep canonical and worst-N fixtures versioned with generator parameters and camera semantics.

## Promotion candidates

- Procedural visual QA should test **gameplay visibility contracts**, not only renderer correctness.
- Road validity requires topology + geometry + dynamics + semantics.
- Critical landmark/junction visibility should be route-weighted and temporal.
- Visual regression should include semantic/depth/navigation buffers, not RGB alone.
- Adversarial camera search should join adversarial seed search for 3D generators.
