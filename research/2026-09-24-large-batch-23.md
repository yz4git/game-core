## New abstractions

1. Observation: modern football AI repeatedly improves off-ball support and positioning. Mechanism: uncontrolled players create future actions before input. Principle: teammate AI quality is option quality, not animation naturalness. Transfer: squad games should measure cover/flank/rescue/combo opportunities. Playtest: how many useful next actions exist because of teammate movement?

2. Observation: runs away from the ball can create space. Mechanism: movement reallocates defenders and opens lanes without possession. Principle: AI contribution includes indirect state change. Transfer: track aggro displacement, lane opening and threat pinning. Playtest: does off-target movement materially benefit the player?

3. Observation: FC 25 combines team tactics and player roles. Mechanism: global shape constrains local behavior. Principle: resolve team objective → role → local action. Transfer: formations plus role-specific utility scoring. Playtest: can a local action be explained by current team intent?

4. Observation: roles alter positioning and movement expectations. Mechanism: roles narrow behavioral distributions and improve predictability. Principle: a role is a behavioral contract, not a stat label. Transfer: support/scout/tank roles should change risk and positioning. Playtest: can role be inferred from decisions with labels hidden?

5. Observation: football AI uses spatial analysis; TacticAI models player relations geometrically. Mechanism: useful action depends on lanes, pressure and relations. Principle: team AI should reason over relational space, not nearest-target distance alone. Transfer: expose open angle, congestion, cover and escape-lane features. Playtest: do mistakes cluster where relational features are missing?

6. Observation: FIFA 22 increased decision frequency while retaining role/personality/teamwork. Mechanism: frequent replanning improves response but can oscillate. Principle: separate fast local adjustment from slower intent commitment. Transfer: add hysteresis/minimum commitment to run, cover and target decisions. Playtest: how often does AI cancel a useful action before value appears?

7. Observation: support movement begins before possession changes. Mechanism: waiting for explicit commands makes assistance late. Principle: cooperative AI should prepare probable future states. Transfer: companions pre-position near cover, combo range or escape routes. Playtest: is the ally already useful when needed?

8. Observation: FIFA 13 made first touch context-dependent instead of universally perfect. Mechanism: imperfect reception restores value to approach angle, pressure and preparation. Principle: removing automatic perfection can create meaningful setup decisions. Transfer: landing, weapon-ready and interaction systems can retain contextual setup costs. Playtest: does guaranteed execution erase an earlier positioning choice?

9. Observation: FC 25 Smart Tactics surfaces contextual tactical options. Mechanism: recommendations expose hidden team-state interpretation while preserving choice. Principle: assistance should externalize a reasoned option rather than silently take agency. Transfer: show press/retreat/focus suggestions with predicted effect. Playtest: can players understand and deliberately reject the suggestion?

10. Observation: team-style systems seek distinct tactical movement under common rules. Mechanism: different role/tactic priors change movement distributions. Principle: AI personality should be measured behaviorally. Transfer: compare heatmaps, risk profiles and action frequencies across teams. Playtest: can teams be distinguished with names/art hidden?

11. Observation: team sports repeatedly transfer user control among autonomous agents. Mechanism: bad switching breaks responsibility prediction. Principle: control transfer and autonomous AI must share the same intent model. Transfer: switching should consider threat, direction and assignment, not nearest distance alone. Playtest: can the user predict the selected teammate?

12. Observation: tactical systems coordinate all players, not isolated agents. Mechanism: individually plausible moves can collectively destroy spacing. Principle: validate macro team invariants before micro polish. Transfer: flag clustering, uncovered zones and duplicated assignments. Playtest: was failure individual or a broken team invariant?

13. Observation: TacticAI evaluates alternative player arrangements. Mechanism: counterfactual comparison tests whether positioning changes outcomes. Principle: evaluate AI decisions against nearby alternative states, not only win rate. Transfer: replay a state with one role/position changed and compare option quality. Playtest: would a small positional change create a materially better choice?

14. Observation: sports games across decades use radically different hardware while retaining recognizable sport decisions. Mechanism: authenticity survives abstraction when space, pressure, timing and teamwork keep causal structure. Principle: simulate causal decision structure before visual completeness. Transfer: mobile sports can simplify animation while preserving tactical relations. Playtest: with placeholder art, are sport-specific tactical decisions still recognizable?

## Implementation-ready telemetry
Track useful-option creation, support angle, spacing histogram, duplicated assignments, uncovered critical-zone time, premature action cancellation, role/action distributions, off-ball-run outcome, switch prediction error, suggestion acceptance, team heatmaps and congestion. Build deterministic scenario tests that vary one role/position/pressure variable and compare counterfactual option quality.
